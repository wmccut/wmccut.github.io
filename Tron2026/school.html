Cheating + AI: Acceptability

ChatGPT publicly released in November 2022, the middle of our 2A term. PD TA's still flinch when they hear the letters A and I.

Given the nature of our degree and AI's rapidly evolving place in modern technology, I figured it'd make sense to ask a few more questions about it.

It seems like most people think self grading with it is chill.

How did you use AI in school?

It seems like a lot people prefer to use AI on topics they feel uninformed about, like for learning course content or finding a starting point for research.

That research part is a big one, as in the past few years Google has automatically enabled AI on all searches, making it difficult for users NOT to use it as a research starting point.

The most popular use case, however, is on things people don't want to do or lack the time for. Proofreading/correcting with it is pretty popular as well.

Stance on AI Applications in Various Fields

While the class seems to lean towards the pro-AI stance in general, its use in the Arts and Defence is still a hot topic.
